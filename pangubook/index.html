Privacy Policy

Personal money management and shared expenses

Effective date: 9 November 2026 · Applies to Pangubook on Android and iOS

Pangubook (the “App”) helps people record income and expenses, manage accounts, and share expenses with selected friends, family members or travel companions. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used and shared, and how you can request access to or deletion of your information.

Want to delete your Pangubook account and associated data?

You can send a deletion request from your registered email address. See Account and data deletion for the steps and what happens next.

1. Who operates Pangubook

Pangubook is developed and operated by Thirvu Soft Private Limited (“we”, “our” or “us”). For privacy questions or requests, contact admin@thirvusoft.com.

2. Information processed by the App

  • Account and identity information: the email address, display name, account identifier, and information needed to establish and maintain a login. A profile picture may be available when supplied through a supported login provider or added by you.
  • Financial records you enter: transaction amounts, dates, categories, income and expense descriptions, currencies, exchange-rate settings, account or ledger labels, notes and related bookkeeping information. These are records you enter, not necessarily verified bank balances or banking transactions.
  • Shared expense data: group names, selected participants, who paid, the split method, amounts owed, settlements, invitations, QR or sharing links where supported, and related group activity.
  • Optional content and permissions: if your version offers attachments or QR scanning and you use them, the App processes the images, documents or camera input needed for that feature. It may also process information you intentionally share using your device's sharing features.
  • Technical information: authentication tokens, basic service logs and technical data necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the App. The particular information processed by third-party services depends on the services enabled in the installed version.

3. Email/password and Google Sign-In

Pangubook supports both email-and-password login and Google Sign-In. Authentication uses Firebase Authentication. For Google Sign-In, Google verifies your Google account and provides the App with permitted account details such as your email address, name and a unique sign-in identifier. Pangubook does not ask you to disclose your Google password to the App.

For email/password login, the password is handled by the authentication service to verify access. Google services are separately governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Why we use information

  • To register and authenticate users, and protect their accounts.
  • To save, retrieve, organize and synchronize personal transactions and account records.
  • To create groups, calculate shares and settlements, and show balances to the relevant participants.
  • To provide customer support, fix problems and maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations where required.

5. Sharing with other users and service providers

Group members: when you participate in a shared group, the group information necessary to calculate expenses and balances may be visible to other members of that group. Do not enter sensitive personal information in group names, notes or attachments unless you are comfortable sharing it with the relevant group members.

Service providers: Pangubook uses Google/Firebase services to provide authentication and cloud functionality. The service provider may process information needed to operate these functions, subject to its agreements and privacy practices. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Other sharing: a group invite or export you intentionally share through another application, such as your device's share sheet, will be handled by that destination application under its own privacy practices. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

6. Storage and security

App account information and synchronized financial or group records are processed using Firebase-backed services. Firebase describes encryption in transit and encryption at rest for supported services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. We apply access controls appropriate to the App's features; however, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

Depending on the configured Firebase service locations and providers, information may be processed outside your state or country.

7. Retention, access and account deletion

We keep account information and expense records for as long as needed to provide the App and meet applicable legal obligations. Uninstalling Pangubook does not automatically delete an online account or its synchronized records.

How to request deletion: Email admin@thirvusoft.com with the subject “Pangubook Account and Data Deletion Request”. Preferably send the email from the address linked to your Pangubook account. If you cannot access that address, tell us and we will explain how to verify account ownership. You do not need to install the App again to contact us.

What deletion covers: After verifying the request, we will process deletion of your Pangubook account and personal data associated with it, subject to information that must be kept to meet legal obligations or legitimate security requirements. We will explain any data that cannot be deleted and confirm when the request has been completed.

Shared group records: An expense or settlement may involve several people. Some shared transaction details may need to remain available to other participants to preserve their group balances. Where retention is necessary, we will limit associated personal information as appropriate. We will explain the handling of your specific shared records when processing a deletion request.

You can also ask to access or correct personal data by writing to the same email address.

8. Your privacy choices

You can choose what financial information to enter and what to share with a group. You can manage optional device permissions using your phone's settings. For questions about stored information, corrections or deletion, contact us using the details below. Turning off device permissions does not itself delete previously stored account or transaction data.

9. Device permissions and third-party links

The App requests a device permission only when needed for an available feature—for example, camera access for a QR scan or photo access for an attachment, where those features are implemented. You can control permissions in your device settings. External websites and applications have their own privacy policies.

10. Children's privacy

Pangubook is a general expense-management tool and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the situation.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Pangubook changes. We will revise the effective date when the policy changes and communicate material changes where required.
